## Successful fresh generations

Fresh short prompts without a checkerboard source generated real alpha. All three production PNGs are 2172×724 RGBA, alpha range 0..255, copied unchanged into `public/redesign/language-garden/`. They have subtle included shadows and text-free opaque centres. No raster postprocessing performed.
